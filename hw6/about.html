About

Competent, compassionate and comprehensive care

Our mission is to improve the quality of life of our patients. The clinic in Bishkek opened in October 2023 to continue and expand our ministry. The outreach work started more than ten years ago; the fixed-site clinic opened later, in October 2023.

Family-oriented

Care for the whole family in one place — no need for expensive specialist consultations.

Evidence-based medicine

We follow evidence-based methods and try to limit unnecessary additional investigations.

Local & international specialists

Family medicine, pediatrics, gynecology, pulmonology and ultrasound.

Training & skill development

We provide training for doctors and medical students.

Supporting medical education

The clinic’s work includes sharing evidence-based practice with doctors and students.
